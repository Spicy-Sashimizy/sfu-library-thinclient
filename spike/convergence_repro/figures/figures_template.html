Regime map: where the artifact lives, on every host measured

Every cell of the seven figures above, placed by its memory cap divided by that host's own measured hot set, so three machines with nothing else in common share one axis. Below the lower boundary the hot set cannot fit and nothing converges; above the upper boundary the whole footprint fits and there is nothing left to sort; the shaded band between them is the only region where the fixed-window inversion and the MGLRU asymmetry can exist at all.
